BatchBaker

Recipe and pan scaling · Android 8.0+

  • Enter named recipes with decimal or fractional ingredient quantities and clearly labeled units.
  • Scale by servings or round/rectangle pan area while keeping batter depth constant.
  • Export scaled cook sheets and CSV, save/edit recipes, and export/restore backups. No generic weight-to-volume conversion; cooking times require judgment.
