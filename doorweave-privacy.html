DoorWeave Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

DoorWeave is published by Technical Dost Solutions. For privacy questions, contact support@technicaldost.com.

This policy describes DoorWeave 1.0 (build 2). The earlier internal build 1 automatically made a session visible when a user tapped Resume or Add a door and started discovery when a user tapped Join a nearby session. Build 1 also used Android's default app backup behavior, so records and preferences from an installation of that build may have been backed up or transferred. Build 2 requires an explicit visibility or discovery tap and opts out of app backup with transfer exclusions. Updating does not remove copies already made by an earlier backup, export or connected phone.

Information kept on your phone

DoorWeave stores the venue and door names you enter, capacity number, session code, count events and timestamps, optional short notes, connection gaps and saved venue presets on your phone. It creates a random installation identifier to distinguish count events made by each phone. DoorWeave does not require an account for counting.

Organizer purchases are handled by Google Play. DoorWeave asks Play for available products and current purchases. It uses a purchase token to acknowledge an eligible completed purchase and stores the last verified unlock status on this phone for offline use. Google Play handles payment details; DoorWeave does not receive card details.

Nearby connections

Opening, starting or resuming a session does not automatically make it visible. If you tap “Make this session visible nearby,” other nearby DoorWeave phones looking for sessions can see the venue name, session code and session identifier before a connection is approved. A phone that requests a connection supplies its door display name. Check that the four digit pairing numbers match on both phones before approving.

After both phones approve, they automatically exchange existing and future session events and synchronization information over a Nearby connection. This can include events originally recorded on other doors and later forwarded by an approved phone. The logs contain the venue name, session code, session identifier, door names, phone installation identifiers, event identifiers, times, count changes and notes. Each connected phone keeps its own copy. “Look for nearby sessions” starts discovery only when you tap it.

Google Play services may collect Nearby connection performance information, including discovery and connection speed, reliability and throughput, along with the device model, country, software build and DoorWeave package name. Google uses this information to improve Nearby. Android's Google Usage & diagnostics settings may provide a control for this collection.

Files, backup and retention

You choose where to save exported logs and reports through the Android file picker. A JSON log export includes the venue name, session code and identifier, plus the session events and notes held on that phone, which may include events from other doors. If you choose a cloud or sharing destination, that destination handles the file under its own terms. Imported logs are copied into the selected local session after you confirm the merge.

DoorWeave opts out of Android app backup and declares exclusions for cloud backup and device transfers. The behavior of device transfer can vary by Android version and manufacturer. These settings do not remove copies from existing backups, exported files or other connected phones.

Local records stay on your phone until you clear DoorWeave's app storage or remove the app. Clear exported files separately at their saved destination. Clearing local storage does not erase copies on other phones or cancel a Google Play subscription. Google Play retains purchase records under its own terms.

Permissions and contact

Nearby connections need Bluetooth, nearby Wi-Fi or legacy location permissions depending on Android version. DoorWeave uses them for finding and connecting doors; it does not have a feature that reads your coordinates. You can count on one phone without enabling nearby access.

For questions about this policy, email support@technicaldost.com.
