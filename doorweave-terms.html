DoorWeave helps staff record entry and exit counts on one or more phones. Counts depend on staff actions, device clocks, connectivity and successful merging. Capacity is a number you enter for reference. DoorWeave does not certify a venue's occupancy or replace required safety procedures. Check the count with staff at each door before relying on a report.

You are responsible for the venue information, notes and files you enter or share. Make a session visible only when you want nearby DoorWeave users to discover its venue name, session code and session identifier. Compare the four digit pairing numbers and approve only known phones. Approved phones automatically exchange existing and future session logs, including events and notes from other doors and phone identifiers; each phone keeps its own copy. JSON log exports include the venue and session identifiers as well as the events held on the exporting phone.
