HearthList privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026. Developer: Technical Dost Solutions (Siddhant Sharma). Contact: support@technicaldost.com.

Local records

HearthList stores the information you enter in private app storage on your device. There is no account, cloud service, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK in this version. The app does not request Internet access. We do not receive your saved records.

Photos and documents

You may select reference photos using Android’s picker. Compact copies are stored locally and included in your JSON backup; originals are not uploaded. Export, backup, import and sharing use Android’s document picker and share sheet. Only files you select are read. When you share or save an export, its data leaves app storage to the recipient or destination you choose, which may be a cloud service with its own policy. Exports can contain personal or customer information.

Permissions and backups

No broad storage, location, contacts, microphone or camera permission is requested. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Keep manual backups securely and delete exported files from their destinations when no longer needed.

Deletion and retention

Records stay until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. Deleting a record removes its locally saved contents; previously exported copies require separate deletion. There is no developer-held account to delete. Restore replaces saved records only after confirmation.

Purchases

Google Play processes paid downloads and may collect payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy. This app contains no billing, ad or payment SDK and receives no payment credentials.

Support and website

If you email support, we receive the details you voluntarily send and use them to answer your request. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information. These public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process routine request metadata under GitHub’s privacy policy; no analytics script is included. The app itself does not send website traffic unless you open a link.

Children and changes

The tool is intended for a general audience; it is not designed to solicit children’s personal information. Material changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and the app’s disclosures before release.
