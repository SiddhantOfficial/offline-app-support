HearthList

Home inventory · Android 8.0+

  • Record belongings by room with serials, notes, value, currency and purchase date.
  • Import compact reference photos; search names, rooms and serials. Keep original receipts/photos separately.
  • Export CSV details and JSON backups with reference photos. Inventory storage is limited to 5 MB. Values are user records, not insurance valuations.

Core tools work offline without registration. Version 1.1.0 and later includes banner advertising and privacy choices; advertising requires internet access. Google Play pricing is configured as free with optional banner ads. Public availability remains subject to release approval; advertising availability depends on Google’s account and app review. No subscription or in-app purchases.
