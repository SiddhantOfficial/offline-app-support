PracticeLedger

Deliberate music practice · Android 8.0+

  • Organize repertoire and target minutes, and log sessions per piece with local dates and notes.
  • Correct or backdate entries, reorder routines, and view recent practice totals.
  • Share CSV practice reports and export/restore a local backup. Sessions are manually logged; no microphone or background timer.

Core tools work offline without registration. Version 1.1.0 (version code 2) is being prepared with banner advertising and privacy choices; advertising requires internet access. Availability and any price will appear in Google Play. No subscription or in-app purchases.
