QuiltFinish

Quilt fabric planning · Android 8.0+

  • Calculate straight-grain binding strips, vertical-panel backing and batting from quilt dimensions.
  • Switch inches and centimeters, add purchase allowance and save named plans.
  • Export the shopping plan and back up saved projects. Assumptions are visible; excludes pattern matching and quilt-top piecing.

Core tools work offline without registration. Version 1.1.0 (version code 2) includes banner advertising and privacy choices; advertising requires internet access. Planned distribution is a free download with optional banner ads. Actual free pricing and availability must be confirmed in Google Play. No subscription or in-app purchases.
