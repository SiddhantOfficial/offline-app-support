ShiftSheet privacy policy

This policy covers ShiftSheet: Daily Site Report for iPhone, developed by Technical Dost Solutions. Updated November 14, 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

ShiftSheet stores your jobs, crew names and hours, equipment, delays, report text, photos and signatures locally. The app does not require a ShiftSheet account, send this material to the developer, collect usage analytics or show ads.

If you take a site photo, ShiftSheet asks for camera access. You can also select an existing image. The photos you add become part of your local report. Optional reminders use local notifications on your iPhone.

Reports you choose to share

When you choose Share PDF, you select the app or recipient that receives the report. Save to Files and Backup to Files save to the location you choose. A report or backup saved to a cloud provider may sync under that provider’s settings. Check a report for personal details before sharing it.

Purchases

Apple handles optional Pro subscriptions and billing. ShiftSheet uses Apple’s StoreKit to read available plans, check subscription access and restore eligible purchases. ShiftSheet does not ask for payment card details.

Removing your data

Deleting the app removes its local reports from that iPhone. Copies you exported, backed up or shared remain at their destinations. Delete those files separately where you saved them.

Privacy questions

Email support@technicaldost.com. Your message and any attachments you choose to send are available to the developer to answer your request. You can ask a privacy question without sending a job report.
