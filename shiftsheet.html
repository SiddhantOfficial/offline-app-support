ShiftSheet support

Need help with a daily site report? Email support@technicaldost.com. Include your iOS version, the screen you were using, and what happened. Please leave out crew names, job addresses, photos and report contents.

Send a report

Review the PDF, then choose Share PDF to select a recipient or app. Choose Save to Files if you want to keep a copy. A PDF is a report you can read and send. To move editable reports to another iPhone, use a backup.

Back up or move your reports

Before deleting ShiftSheet or changing phones, open Settings in ShiftSheet and choose Backup to Files. Save the backup somewhere you can access from the other phone.

On the destination iPhone, choose Restore from Files and select the backup. Check the summary. Add to current data keeps existing reports. Replace after local safety copy saves a local copy first and then replaces the current data.

Restore Pro access

Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account associated with the purchase. Manage or cancel your subscription in the iPhone Settings app under your name, then Subscriptions.
