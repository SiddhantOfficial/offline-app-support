Support

Email support@technicaldost.com. This is the support address already used by the developer’s existing Android app.

Include the app name, Android version, app version and steps that reproduce the problem. Please avoid sending private records, customer details or receipts unless needed and you choose to share them.

Protect your data

Saved records remain on the device. Export a JSON backup to a location you control before changing devices, clearing app storage or uninstalling. Restore replaces current records after confirmation; export current records first. Corrupt or incompatible backups are rejected.

Exports

Export opens Android’s document picker. Share opens available apps with a document attachment. If no recipient app is installed, use Export. CSV exports escape spreadsheet formulas in text fields.

Advertising privacy

Version 1.1.0 (version code 2) is being prepared with Google AdMob banner advertising. Core tools remain usable offline; ads require internet. Where Google requires it, use “Ad privacy choices” in the app to review advertising choices. See the app’s privacy policy for the distinction between local records and advertising data. Include the app version when reporting an advertising issue.

Payments

Purchases, refunds and reinstallation are handled by Google Play when an app is publicly available. Keep the same Google account for reinstallation. Availability and prices depend on the store region.
