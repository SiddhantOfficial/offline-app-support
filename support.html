Support

Email support@technicaldost.com. This is the support address already used by the developer’s existing Android app.

Include the app name, Android version, app version and steps that reproduce the problem. Please avoid sending private records, customer details or receipts unless needed and you choose to share them.

Protect your data

Saved records remain on the device. Export a JSON backup to a location you control before changing devices, clearing app storage or uninstalling. Restore replaces current records after confirmation; export current records first. Corrupt or incompatible backups are rejected.

Exports

Export opens Android’s document picker. Share opens available apps with a document attachment. If no recipient app is installed, use Export. CSV exports escape spreadsheet formulas in text fields.

Advertising privacy

Version 1.1.0 and later includes optional Google AdMob banner advertising. Core tools remain usable offline; ads require internet. Where Google requires it, use “Ad privacy choices” in the app to review advertising choices. See the app’s privacy policy for the distinction between local records and advertising data. Include the app version when reporting an advertising issue.

The redesigned tools

FieldQuote uses an estimating workspace; HearthList organizes room and photo inventory; QuiltFinish provides a measurement and fabric-diagram workbench; PracticeLedger shows weekly music practice; BatchBaker provides a recipe book and scaling flow. All retain local records, exports and JSON backup/restore.

Availability and pricing

Free downloads with optional banner advertising are planned. Actual pricing and availability must be verified in Google Play. No subscription or in-app purchases are integrated. Store transactions, refunds and reinstallation, where applicable, are handled by Google Play.
